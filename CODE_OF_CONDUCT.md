# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the overall community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, threats, or retaliation against a reporter.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission, including
  contact details, financial records, account identifiers, or credentials.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Discuss Spendly's code, research, and financial assumptions respectfully.
Challenge claims with evidence rather than judging someone's financial situation
or level of experience. Use synthetic examples in public discussions.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Spendly's repository maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
these standards and taking appropriate, fair corrective action in response to
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful behavior.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits,
issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
They will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate, while
protecting affected people's privacy.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project community spaces, including
issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related discussions. It also applies
when someone officially represents Spendly in public, online or offline.

## Reporting and Enforcement

No dedicated confidential conduct-reporting address is currently published.
To request one, open a minimal issue in the
[Spendly issue tracker](https://github.com/Eeshan-Vaghjiani/Spendly/issues)
titled **Confidential conduct contact requested**. Include only the request for
a private channel; do not post names, allegations, incident evidence, or private
information. Wait for a maintainer to arrange a private channel before sending
details. Do not assume GitHub issues or comments are private.

For abuse on GitHub, including concerns involving a maintainer or situations
where contacting the project is inappropriate, use
[GitHub's abuse-reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
GitHub handles reports under its own policies; this is not a project-managed inbox.
Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports fairly, share incident details only as needed
to investigate and act, and respect reporters' privacy and security. A maintainer
involved in a complaint should not adjudicate it. Where another uninvolved
maintainer is available, request their review of a decision through the agreed
private channel. No fixed response time or independent appeals team is promised.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will use these community-impact guidelines, taking severity and
repeated behavior into account. Serious incidents may warrant immediate removal.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact:** Inappropriate language or other unprofessional or
unwelcome behavior.

**Consequence:** A private, written warning explaining the violation and why
the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact:** A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence:** A warning with consequences for continued behavior. For a
specified period, no unsolicited interaction with the people involved, including
those enforcing this code, in community spaces or external channels. Violating
these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community impact:** A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate
behavior.

**Consequence:** A temporary ban from interaction or public communication with
the community for a specified period. No unsolicited public or private contact
with the people involved is allowed during that period. Violating these terms
may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community impact:** A pattern of violations, including sustained inappropriate
behavior, harassment, or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.

**Consequence:** A permanent ban from public interaction within the community.
