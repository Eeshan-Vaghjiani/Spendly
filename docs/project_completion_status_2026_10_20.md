## Definition of finished

The approved requirements are mapped to demonstrated behavior; final source and
APK have recorded identities; selected-model limitations are disclosed; app and
admin checks pass on the stated targets; remaining external integrations are
explicitly out of scope or signed off. The final report must distinguish mocked
unit coverage, actual portable inference, Android execution, disposable PostgreSQL
and read-only hosted checks. No claim of production readiness or universal
forecast accuracy is supported by these results.

The original research working tree, notebooks and exports remain untouched.
