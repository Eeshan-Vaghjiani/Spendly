## Verification and limits

[PUBLICATION_REPORT.json](PUBLICATION_REPORT.json) records:

- **13/13 APKs** downloaded without authentication: HTTP 200, exact size and SHA256.
- **10/10 release pages and companion checksums** accessible without authentication.
- **10/10 tags** match the explicitly selected historical source targets.
- `/releases/latest` resolves to v1.4.1; the rendered main-page sidebar shows Latest.

All binaries require Android 7.0/API 24 or later and use the same **Android Debug
certificate**. Publication/Latest is not Google Play or production certification.
Older versions are unsupported educational archives, not recommended real-finance
clients. Source-association gaps, especially 1.3.5+13, remain explicitly disclosed;
an archive tag is not proof of an exactly reproducible APK build. Backend
availability, historical-client compatibility, institutional/privacy/licensing/SMS
approval and fresh device/OAuth acceptance are not established by these checks.

No app builds, signing changes, production database work, training or deployment
were performed. This documentation-only PR records the completed publication;
merging it does not deploy the app or grant further distribution permissions.
[All original APK checksums](SHA256SUMS.txt) are retained in source. Per-build
source associations and provenance limits are disclosed in the public release
notes; the local pre-publication audit remains historical evidence outside this PR.
